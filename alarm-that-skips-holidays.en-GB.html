The bank holiday routine you can drop

A weekday repeat cannot tell a bank holiday from any other Monday. So you switch the alarm off on Sunday night and hope you remember to switch it back on. EasyAM checks each day against holiday data for your country or region and against your own days off, then rings only on working days.

Step by step: set up a workday alarm

  1. Pick your country or region; holiday data for 67 countries and regions is built in, works offline and can be updated.
  2. Create your alarm with a weekly repeat or a one-off time, a label and snooze from 1 to 60 minutes.
  3. Add annual leave, a trip or a sick day as days off, as single days or date ranges.
  4. Teachers and students can add half-term or study leave as days off.
  5. Check the seven-day view (14 days on iPad) before bed.

Fancy a lie-in?

From the day before, tap Sleep in to silence just the next ring while your routine stays as it is. You can do it in the app, from the medium or large Home Screen widget, or from your Apple Watch, which shows done once your iPhone has confirmed it with iOS.

Frequently asked questions

Does it know bank holidays in Scotland or Northern Ireland?

Choose your country or region in the app. Holiday data covers 67 countries and regions, with state or provincial holidays for 15 countries; check the calendar tab to see the dates it will skip.

Will it ring on the Tuesday after a bank holiday?

Yes. Only the holiday itself and the days off you add stay quiet; working days ring as normal.

Can I skip just one morning?

Yes. Sleep in silences only the next ring.

What if holiday data is out of date?

That day counts as a working day, so the alarm rings.
