Holiday data

Sources, confidence labels and licences.

EasyAM ships with a bundled holiday dataset for 67 countries (2026 to 2028) and can download newer files from a separate public host that contains only holiday data (alice51849.github.io/holiday-calendar-data). Official government publications are used wherever they exist.

Confidence labels

  • Verified: checked against an official publication for that year.
  • Rule derived: computed from statutory rules or an open-source rules engine, because no official calendar for that year was available.
  • Uncertain: sources disagree, or the date depends on an announcement or a moon sighting. An uncertain date never silences an alarm by default.
