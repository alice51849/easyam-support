What it is

EasyAM is an alarm clock app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch that decides day by day whether your alarm should ring. It rings on workdays and stays quiet on public holidays and on days off you add yourself. A seven-day preview (14 days in the wide iPad layout) shows which mornings ring and at what time.

Who it is for

  • Office workers who are tired of switching alarms off before a long weekend and back on after it.
  • Students and teachers: ring on class days, add school breaks as time off.
  • Shift workers: set a shift or alternating-week pattern once (with the in-app purchase).
  • People working abroad or moving: change country or region anytime; 67 countries and regions are built in.

How it is priced

EasyAM is a free download with a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase unlocks multiple smart schedules, shift and alternating-week patterns, a holiday calendar for each alarm and 100 more clock themes. It is a single purchase, not a subscription. There are no ads.

What is different about it

  • Holiday data for 67 countries and regions (2026 to 2028), usable offline, including make-up working days where a country has them.
  • If holiday data for a day is unknown or outdated, the day counts as a workday, so the alarm still rings.
  • Uses real iOS system alarms, marked ready only after iOS confirms the schedule, with a schedule health screen.
  • One tap skips only tomorrow’s ring and keeps your routine; you can also do it from a widget or Apple Watch.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking; alarms and settings stay on your device.

FAQ

Does EasyAM ring on public holidays?

No. Public and regional holidays are skipped automatically, and it rings as usual on workdays, including make-up working days in places that have them.

Is EasyAM free?

It is a free download. Multiple smart schedules, shift and alternating-week patterns, a holiday calendar for each alarm and 100 more clock themes are unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase.

Can I add my own vacation days?

Yes. Add a single day or a date range as time off, with your own label and icon, and an optional wake-up alarm for that time off.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

Yes. The Apple Watch app installs with the iPhone app, shows your next alarm and the coming seven days, and lets you skip tomorrow’s ring from your wrist. Your iPhone schedules the alarms.

Does it need an account or collect data?

No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only network use is downloading public holiday data files.
