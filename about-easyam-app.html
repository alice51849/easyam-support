What this app is

EasyAM decides day by day whether your alarm should ring: it rings on workdays and stays quiet on public holidays and the days off you add. Open the app and the next seven days show which mornings ring and at what time.

  • Public and regional holidays are skipped automatically, with holiday data for 67 countries and regions for 2026 to 2028, usable offline
  • Vacations, sick days and trips stay quiet once you add them as time off (a single day or a date range)
  • Make-up working days ring as usual in places that have them; days with unknown or outdated holiday data count as workdays, so the alarm rings
  • Change the time for just one day, or skip only the next ring with one tap
  • Real iOS system alarms, plus widgets, Lock Screen widgets, StandBy and an Apple Watch app
  • No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking; the only network use is downloading public holiday data files

Who it's for

  • Office workers who are tired of turning alarms off before a long weekend and back on after it
  • Students and teachers who want to ring on class days and add school breaks as time off
  • Shift workers who want a shift or alternating-week pattern set once (in-app purchase)
  • People working abroad or moving: change your country or region anytime

How pricing works

EasyAM is free to download. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks multiple smart schedules, shift and alternating-week patterns, a holiday calendar for each alarm and 100 more clock themes. Check the App Store page for the current price in your country.

FAQ

What does EasyAM do?

It rings on workdays and stays quiet on public holidays and the days off you add, so you don't have to switch alarms off before long weekends.

Which countries are supported?

Holiday data for 67 countries and regions for 2026 to 2028 is built in and works offline; you can change your country or region anytime.

What if holiday data for a day is missing?

Days with unknown or outdated holiday data count as workdays, so the alarm rings.

Is it free?

Yes, it is free to download. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks multiple smart schedules, shift and alternating-week patterns, per-alarm holiday calendars and 100 more clock themes.

Does it work with Apple Watch?

Yes. The Apple Watch app shows your next alarm and the coming seven days, and you can skip tomorrow's ring from your wrist.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no ads, analytics or tracking, and your alarms and settings stay on your device.

Is there an Android version?

Yes, EasyAM is on the App Store and Google Play.
