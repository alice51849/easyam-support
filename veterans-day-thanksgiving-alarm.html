Veterans Day and Thanksgiving: sleep in on the holiday, wake up for work

November brings two US holidays close together: Veterans Day on Wednesday, November 11, and Thanksgiving on Thursday, November 26. You want a quiet morning on the day off and a reliable alarm the next workday. Here is how EasyAM: Holiday Alarm Clock handles each one. Answers are based on the App Store listing.

Veterans Day (Nov 11): check your list, then match your schedule

Many people work on Veterans Day while others get it off, so check before you rely on it. EasyAM skips US public holidays in the app's holiday list automatically. Open the Calendar tab and see whether November 11 is marked as a public holiday.

  • You have the day off, and it is not marked: add November 11 as time off. The alarm stays quiet that morning.
  • You work that day, and it is marked: add it as an extra working day, so the alarm rings as usual.
  • Thursday the 12th: open the app the night before. Your next seven days show which mornings ring and at what time.

Thanksgiving (Nov 26): nothing to switch off

Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day mornings stay quiet on their own. There is no alarm to turn off on Wednesday night and nothing to remember to turn back on. Hosting dinner and need an early start? Change the ring time for just that day; the rest of the week stays the same.

Taking the Friday after off?

Add Friday, November 27 as time off, for a single day or a date range, with your own label and icon. Taking the whole week? Add the date range once, and edit or remove it at once if plans change. Flying out early? Time off can have its own optional wake-up alarm. Monday morning rings as usual.

Your next alarm also appears on your widgets, Lock Screen and Apple Watch, so a quick glance Sunday night tells you Monday is covered.

Frequently asked questions

Will EasyAM stay quiet on Thanksgiving morning?

Yes. Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day mornings stay quiet on their own and the alarm rings again on the next workday.

Does it skip Veterans Day on November 11?

Public and regional holidays for your country are skipped automatically, but not every employer gives Veterans Day off. Open the Calendar tab and check whether November 11 is marked as a public holiday. If you have the day off and it is not marked, add it as time off. If you work that day and it is marked, add it as an extra working day.

How do I take the Friday after Thanksgiving off?

Add that Friday as time off, for a single day or a date range, with your own label and icon. The alarm stays quiet and rings again on Monday.

How do I make sure it rings the morning after the holiday?

Open the app the night before. The next seven days show which mornings ring and at what time. Days with unknown or outdated holiday data count as workdays, so the alarm rings.
