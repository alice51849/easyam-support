Common questions

My alarm did not ring

Check that the alarm is on, that alarm permission is allowed for the app in iOS Settings, that the day is not a skipped holiday or time off (the Calendar tab shows how each day is treated), and that Settings shows Alarms Ready. Then write to us with the details.

How far ahead are my alarms set?

Open Settings and look at the schedule health card: "Covered through" shows the last day with a confirmed alarm. Opening the app extends it.

A holiday is wrong or missing

Email us the country, the date and an official source link. Dates the app cannot confirm are treated as workdays, so a workday alarm is never skipped because of missing data.

Why does it not say "Alarms ready"?

That message appears only after iOS has confirmed your upcoming alarms. Check that alarm permission is allowed, then use "Check again" in Settings.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings and tap "Restore Purchases" in the Pro card (the same button is on the upgrade screen). Use the Apple ID you bought with.

How do I delete my data?

There is no account. Delete alarms inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device.

Which language does it use?

The app follows your system language. Change it in iOS Settings, General, Language and Region, or per app in the app's Settings page.

Widgets look out of date

Open the app once so it can refresh its status; widgets show what the app last confirmed.
