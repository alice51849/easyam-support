Why weekday repeats break on a rotation

On a rotation your working days drift across the week. Every new cycle means editing alarms by hand, and one missed edit is a missed shift or a 5 a.m. wake-up on your day off. EasyAM plans the days from a pattern you enter once, and you check the result in the seven-day preview (14 days on iPad) instead of in your head.

Step by step: set up a shift alarm

  1. Create your alarm with the time you need and a label, for example Early shift.
  2. Unlock shift and alternating-week patterns with the one-time in-app purchase, then set your pattern once.
  3. Add extra working days such as overtime, business trips, night shifts or on-call days, with labels and icons.
  4. Add vacation and sick days as time off so those mornings stay quiet.
  5. Check the seven-day preview or the calendar tab to confirm which days ring.

Public holidays still handled

Holiday data for 67 countries and regions is built in. With the in-app purchase, each alarm can follow its own holiday calendar, which helps if one alarm is for work and another is for something that ignores holidays. If holiday data for a day is missing or out of date, that day counts as a workday and the alarm rings.

Frequently asked questions

Are shift patterns included in the download?

The download includes one alarm. Shift and alternating-week patterns, unlimited alarms, a holiday calendar for each alarm and 100 more clock themes come with a one-time in-app purchase.

Can I add night shifts and on-call days?

Yes. Add them as extra working days with your own label and icon, as single days or date ranges.

Can I change the time for just one shift?

Yes. Give one day, or several in a row, its own ring time; the rest stay the same.

Does it work with my Apple Watch?

The watch app shows your next alarm and the coming seven days and can skip tomorrow's ring. Your iPhone schedules the alarms, so it works with your paired iPhone.
