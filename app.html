The app

A smart alarm clock that knows your days off.

Most alarms repeat on fixed weekdays, so you have to switch them off before every holiday and remember to switch them back on. EasyAM works out the days you really need to wake up and schedules real system alarms only for those days.

How it works

  1. You set your working days (or a shift pattern), wake-up time, country and region once.
  2. EasyAM checks the holiday calendar for your region: public and regional holidays are skipped, make-up workdays are added and your own time off is respected.
  3. It schedules the upcoming rings as individual alarms with Apple's AlarmKit, so iOS itself rings them.
  4. It reads the schedule back from iOS and shows "Next alarm" and "Alarms ready" only after iOS has confirmed them.

Features

  • Public holidays and make-up workdays for 67 countries, each date labelled with how confident the data is
  • Personal time off, for one alarm or for all of them
  • Weekly, alternating-week and shift-cycle repeat patterns
  • "Sleep in tomorrow" skips only the next ring and leaves your rule untouched
  • Schedule health with a "Covered through" date, so you can see how far ahead your alarms are set
  • A holiday calendar showing public holidays, make-up workdays and time off
  • 100 clock themes you can preview before unlocking
  • Widgets, Lock Screen widgets and a countdown Live Activity
  • An Apple Watch app: see your next alarm and the coming days synced from your iPhone, choose "Sleep in tomorrow" from your wrist, and add watch-face complications. The iPhone schedules and rings the alarms; the watch needs the paired iPhone.
  • Layouts for iPhone, iPad and wide windows; follows your system language

Free and Pro

The free version includes one saved alarm with holiday skipping, make-up workdays, time off, the next-alarm preview and one clock theme. Pro is a one-time in-app purchase (there is no subscription) that unlocks unlimited alarms, 100 clock themes, advanced repeat patterns and a holiday calendar for each alarm. The price is shown by the App Store in your currency.

Good to know

  • iOS limits how many alarms an app can schedule, so EasyAM keeps a rolling window of upcoming rings and extends it whenever you open the app. "Covered through" shows how far it reaches.
  • Governments sometimes announce holidays late. A date the app cannot confirm is treated as a workday, so uncertain data never silences a workday alarm.
  • EasyAM manages only the alarms it creates. It does not control alarms in Apple's Clock app.
  • For anything critical, keep a backup alarm.
