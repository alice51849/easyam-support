EasyAM

Set once. Sleep in on every holiday.

An alarm clock that knows your days off. It rings on the days you need to wake up and stays quiet on public holidays, make-up workdays and your own time off.

How it worksGet support

How it works

  1. Tell it your week. Working days, wake-up time, country and region.
  2. It plans the days that matter. Public holidays are skipped, make-up workdays are included and your time off is respected.
  3. iOS rings it. Alarms are scheduled with Apple's AlarmKit, and the app shows your next alarm only after iOS has confirmed it.
