Will my holiday alarm go off on the day after Thanksgiving (Black Friday)?

Usually yes. In the US, Thanksgiving itself (Thursday, November 26, 2026) is the federal holiday; Friday, November 27 is not, so EasyAM treats it as a workday and rings unless you tell it otherwise. Many people take that Friday off, and it takes one step to make it quiet.

  1. Check: open the Calendar tab and see whether November 27 is marked as a public holiday for your country or region.
  2. Taking it off: add November 27 as time off, with your own label and icon. Taking the whole week? Add the date range once.
  3. Just sleeping in: one tap skips only the next ring and keeps your routine, from the app, the medium or large Next Alarm widget, or Apple Watch.
  4. Confirm Thursday night: the seven-day preview shows which mornings ring; Monday, November 30 rings as usual.

Will my holiday alarm ring on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve?

Usually yes. In the US, Christmas Day (Friday, December 25, 2026) and New Year's Day (Friday, January 1, 2027) are federal holidays, so EasyAM stays quiet on those mornings. Christmas Eve (Thursday, December 24) and New Year's Eve (Thursday, December 31) are not federal holidays, so EasyAM treats them as workdays and rings unless you add them as time off.

  1. Check: open the Calendar tab and see which December and January dates are marked as public holidays for your country or region.
  2. Taking the Eves off: add December 24 and December 31 as time off, each with your own label and icon.
  3. Taking the whole break: add one date range, for example December 24, 2026 to January 1, 2027, as time off. You can edit or remove the whole range at once if your plans change.
  4. Need to be up for a flight? Time off can have its own optional wake-up alarm.
  5. Just sleeping in once: one tap skips only the next ring and keeps your routine, from the app, the medium or large Next Alarm widget, or Apple Watch.
  6. Confirm the night before: the seven-day preview shows which mornings ring; Monday, January 4, 2027 rings as usual unless you add it too.

Which US federal holidays will my holiday alarm skip in early 2027?

With the United States selected as your country or region, EasyAM stays quiet on these federal holidays from January through May 2027 and rings on the surrounding workdays as usual:

  • New Year's Day: Friday, January 1, 2027
  • Martin Luther King Jr. Day: Monday, January 18, 2027
  • Washington's Birthday: Monday, February 15, 2027
  • Memorial Day: Monday, May 31, 2027

Holiday data for 2026 to 2028 is built in and works offline, so these days are already marked as public holidays in the Calendar tab. If a day's holiday data is unknown or outdated, that day counts as a workday and the alarm rings.

  1. Check: open the Calendar tab to see the public holidays marked for your country or region, and the seven-day preview to see which mornings ring.
  2. Working on one of these holidays? Add it as an extra working day, with your own label and icon, and the alarm rings that morning.
  3. Different start time that day: change the ring time for just that day; the rest stay the same.
  4. Taking the Friday or Tuesday too: add it as time off, or add a date range for the whole long weekend.

Frequently asked questions

What is a holiday alarm clock?

An alarm that knows the public holiday calendar. EasyAM rings on workdays and stays quiet on public holidays and your days off, so you do not have to turn it off before a long weekend and back on after it.

How can I tell tonight whether my alarm will ring tomorrow?

Open the app to see the next seven days with the mornings that ring and their times, or glance at the Next Alarm widget, the Lock Screen widget or the Apple Watch app.

I have to work on a holiday. Can I still get woken up?

Yes. Add that date as an extra working day, such as overtime, a business trip, a night shift or an on-call day, with your own label and icon.

Can it wake me on the morning of a vacation flight?

Yes. Time off can have its own optional wake-up alarm, and a whole date range can be edited or removed at once.

Is it a real alarm?

Yes. EasyAM uses real iOS system alarms, marks them ready only after iOS confirms the schedule, and includes a schedule health screen. A Live Activity and Dynamic Island view appear while an alarm rings or snoozes.
