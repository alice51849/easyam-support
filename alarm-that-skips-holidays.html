Why the built-in weekday alarm is not enough

A weekday repeat rings every Monday to Friday, including federal holidays, company days off and the Friday you took as vacation. So the routine becomes: remember the holiday, switch the alarm off the night before, then remember to switch it back on. Miss the second step and you are late on the first day back.

EasyAM flips that around. You set your wake-up time once, and each morning it checks whether that day is a workday, a public or regional holiday, or a day off you entered. Workdays ring; the rest stay quiet.

Step by step: set up an alarm that skips holidays

  1. Choose your country or region. Holiday data for 67 countries and regions from 2026 to 2028 is built in and works offline.
  2. Create your alarm with a weekly repeat or a one-time ring, a label, and snooze from 1 to 60 minutes.
  3. Add vacations, sick days or trips as time off, for a single day or a date range, with your own label and icon.
  4. Open the app before bed: the seven-day preview (14 days on iPad) shows which mornings ring and when.
  5. Want a slow start tomorrow? One tap skips only the next ring, and your routine stays as it is.

What rings and what stays quiet

That last row matters: when in doubt, EasyAM wakes you. It never silently skips a day it is not sure about.

See tomorrow without opening the app

  • Next Alarm widget in small, medium and large sizes (extra large on iPad), with holiday and schedule status.
  • Medium and large widgets add a one-tap button to sleep in tomorrow.
  • Lock Screen widgets in three styles; the small widget also shows in StandBy.
  • Apple Watch app with your next alarm and the coming seven days, synced from your iPhone.
  • A Live Activity and Dynamic Island view while an alarm rings or snoozes.

Alarms are real iOS system alarms, marked ready only after iOS confirms the schedule, and a schedule health screen shows the state.

Frequently asked questions

Does the iPhone Clock app skip holidays on its own?

A weekday repeat rings on every weekday, holidays included. EasyAM checks each day against holiday data for your country or region and your own days off.

Which holidays does EasyAM know?

Public holidays for 67 countries and regions from 2026 to 2028, including state or provincial holidays for 15 countries. You can change country or region at any time.

What if I take a Friday off?

Add it as time off. That morning stays quiet, and you can give a day off its own wake-up alarm if you still need one, for example for an early flight.

Can I skip just tomorrow?

Yes. One tap skips only the next ring, from the app, the medium or large widget, or your Apple Watch.

What happens if holiday data is missing?

That day counts as a workday and the alarm rings.
