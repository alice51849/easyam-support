Privacy Policy

EasyAM · Effective October 12, 2026

In short. EasyAM has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your alarms and settings stay on your device. The only network traffic the app makes is downloading public holiday data files, and those requests carry no personal information and nothing about your alarms.

1. Who we are

EasyAM is made by an independent developer (“we”). You can reach us at hourstag.app@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

  • Alarms you create: name, time, repeat pattern, personal leave dates and optional labels, snooze setting, sound and clock theme.
  • Settings: the country and region used for holidays, recently used countries, your selected clock theme, whether setup is finished, and a cached copy of whether Pro is unlocked.
  • A small status file used by widgets, Live Activities and the Apple Watch app: next alarm, holiday notice, schedule health, theme, whether Pro is unlocked and your country code.
  • Cached holiday data downloaded earlier.

We run no server for this data and cannot read it. If you back up your device, Apple’s backup terms apply to what is stored on it.

3. Permissions and Apple features

  • Alarms (AlarmKit): the app asks iOS to schedule alarms. iOS keeps and rings them; you can turn the permission off in Settings.
  • Notifications: only if you allow them, the app can show a local reminder generated on your device. There is no push server.
  • Background refresh: iOS may wake the app briefly to keep upcoming alarms up to date.
  • Widgets and Live Activities: show information from the on-device status file.
  • Apple Watch: the iPhone app sends the same status information to the app on your paired Apple Watch through Apple’s WatchConnectivity, and the watch sends “Sleep in tomorrow” requests back to the iPhone. This stays between your own devices; we never receive it.
  • Time zone: used on your device to suggest a country. The app does not use location services.
  • Not used: contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, location, advertising identifier. The app does not read your calendars.

4. Network use

  1. Holiday data updates. The app can download public holiday data files from a public static host that contains only holiday data (https://alice51849.github.io/holiday-calendar-data/). Requests are plain HTTPS GET requests for an index file and for one file per country and year you use. They contain no account, device identifier, cookie or query string, and nothing about your alarms, schedule, calendar or purchases. Because of how the web works, the host receives your IP address and the requested file name, which shows a country code and a year. We do not receive or keep access logs; the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical logs under its own privacy statement. Downloaded files are checked for integrity before use, and the app keeps working offline with the data built in.
  2. Purchases. Handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your Apple ID or payment details; the app only learns whether Pro is unlocked.
  3. Our website is static pages with no analytics, cookies or third-party content.
  4. Email. If you write to us we receive your address and message, use them only to reply, and keep them only as long as needed.

5. What we do not do

We do not create accounts, track you, build profiles, sell or share personal information, show ads, include third-party SDKs or analytics, or upload your content anywhere.

6. Retention and deletion

Everything the app stores is on your device. You can delete alarms and leave dates in the app at any time, and deleting the app removes the data it stored on your device.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Your choices

Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you email us we will help and delete our correspondence on request.

9. Security

Data stays inside the app’s protected storage on your device. Downloads use HTTPS and are validated before they are used.

10. Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its effective date, and mention material changes in the app’s release notes.

11. Android version

This section applies to EasyAM for Android, distributed through Google Play. It adds to, and where it differs replaces, the iOS-specific wording above (AlarmKit, Live Activities, Apple Watch and Apple’s purchase handling do not exist on Android). Everything else is the same: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing about you or your alarms is sent to us.

  • Alarms: the app asks Android’s alarm service (AlarmManager) to ring your alarms at the exact time. On Android 13 and later the exact-alarm permission is granted automatically to alarm-clock apps; on Android 12 you may be asked to allow “Alarms & reminders”. It is used only to ring your alarms on time. When an alarm rings the app can show its ringing screen over the lock screen (full-screen notification permission) so you can stop it or snooze it.
  • Notifications: on Android 13 and later the app asks for permission to show notifications. They are generated on your device for the ringing alarm and for holiday notices; there is no push server.
  • Restart: the app listens for the device-restart broadcast (“receive boot completed”) only to schedule your alarms again after the phone restarts or the time, time zone or language changes.
  • Widgets: the home-screen widgets show the next alarm and holiday notice from a small file stored in the app’s own storage on your device. The “sleep in tomorrow” button on the widget acts only inside the app.
  • Purchases: Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play and handled entirely by Google. We never see your Google account or payment details; the app only learns from Google Play whether Pro is purchased. Google’s own privacy policy applies to Google Play.
  • Rating prompt: the app may occasionally ask Google Play to show its in-app review dialog. Google shows and handles that dialog; the app does not receive your rating or review.
  • Network: the Internet permission is used only to download the public holiday data files described in section 4 and by Google Play for purchases.
  • Backup: your alarms and settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may back this data up to your own Google Account and restore it on a new device. That is done by Android under Google’s terms; we cannot access it, and you can turn it off in your device’s system settings. It replaces the Apple backup sentence in section 2.
  • Not used: contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, the advertising ID, and health data. The app does not read your calendars.
  • Google Play Data safety: the app’s Data safety form states that no user data is collected or shared, which matches this policy.
